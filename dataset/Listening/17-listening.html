Unfortunately, it took three major disasters to bring about this change in attitude. 32All three of these involved the collapse of bridges. The first, known as the Ashtabula River Railroad Disaster, happened in 1876 in the USA, and we now think it was probably caused by metal fatigue.

This incident, which happened early in the 20th century, is known as the Boston Molasses Disaster. At the time, 35molasses was used rather than sugar as the principal sweetener in the USA.
